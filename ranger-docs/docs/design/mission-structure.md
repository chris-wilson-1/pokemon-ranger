# Mission Structure

Each "gym" becomes a **Ranger Base** with a structured mission loop.

## The loop

```
Arrive at base ──▶ Ops Board briefing ──▶ Field deployment ──▶ Debrief ──▶ Rank up
```

### 1. Arrive at base
Ranger Bases are the equivalent of gym towns. They're working buildings — bunks, quartermaster, training yard, ops room. Not just a service stop.

### 2. Ops Board briefing
A scripted NPC at the ops board gives the mission briefing. Replaces the "challenge the gym leader" approach.

The briefing should:
- Set the stakes plainly
- Name the location
- Note any partner NPCs assigned to the mission
- Hand over mission-specific gear if relevant

### 3. Field deployment
The mission itself — a route, dungeon, or interior, with the climax being a battle, rescue, escort, or boss encounter. This is where existing pokeemerald mechanics carry the weight.

### 4. Debrief
Return to the ops board (or a dedicated debrief room). Resolution scene, character beats, sometimes consequences that ripple into later missions.

### 5. Rank up
Triggered at the debrief on milestone missions. See [Rank System](rank-system.md).

## Why this structure

- Gives the world a sense of *institution* — the Ranger Corps is a real organisation with a workflow
- Makes story beats land at predictable rhythm points (briefing, climax, debrief) — easy to write to
- Lets us vary mission *type* without varying mission *shape* — the player always knows where they are in the cycle
- Supports the [tone arc](../vision/tone-and-arc.md): the briefing/debrief frame is wholesome and procedural at first, and becomes the place where the darker material gets delivered later
