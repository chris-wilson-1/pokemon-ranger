# Pillars

The non-negotiables. Everything else is up for negotiation; these aren't.

## 1. Storytelling first
Strong writing matters more than cinematic presentation. We're not chasing Unbound's mugshot/cutscene production budget — we're chasing its narrative depth and the way its plot escalates.

## 2. Wholesome surface, dark core
The opening hours read as a comfortable, low-stakes Pokémon Ranger story. Then it twists — fast and unexpectedly. The contrast is the point. See [Tone & Story Arc](tone-and-arc.md).

## 3. Ranger reframe, mainline mechanics
Standard battle mechanics throughout. Rangers, ranks, missions, gear, and partners are all narrative reskins of trainer/badge/HM/follower systems. We do not try to simulate stylus-capture minigames.

## 4. One challenging difficulty
No mode select. The game is tuned to one curve and that curve has teeth. Players who want vanilla difficulty are not the target audience.

## 5. Frictionless competitive customisation
Perfect IVs on every Pokémon, from wild encounters to gifts to trainer mons (where relevant for the player). EV training, nature mints, and ability patches are easily accessible from early game. Building a team should never be a grind — the challenge is in *fights*, not in *prep*.

## 6. Lean side content (for now)
Side content is deliberately minimal in v1. The bounty/mission-board ambition stays parked until the main path is shippable. One small thing done well > five things done badly.

## 7. Minimal custom presentation
No custom mugshots, no cutscene camera, no UI overhauls. The one exception: rank-up moments get a custom animation/screen — those are emotional beats and deserve presentation budget.
